How Online Puja Works

  1. 1

    Send a Request

    Pick a puja and a time in your local time zone. No payment now.

  2. 2

    We Confirm

    We call or WhatsApp you to confirm, then send the live video link.

  3. 3

    Sankalpam

    The Pandit takes sankalpam with your family names, gothram and nakshatram.

  4. 4

    Join Live

    Watch and take part from home. Offer prayers when the Pandit guides you.

  5. 5

    Prasadam

    Prasadam is packed and shipped internationally, with tracking.
