About Agni Puja

Keeping Sacred Traditions Simple for Modern Families

Agni Puja was started to make it easy for families to perform pujas and homams the right way, even with busy schedules or when living far from their hometown. We connect you with trusted, experienced Vedic priests who perform each ritual with devotion and care.

From a simple Satyanarayana Pooja to a full Gruhapravesam or wedding, we take care of the priest, the muhurtham and the puja samagri – so you can focus on the blessings.
